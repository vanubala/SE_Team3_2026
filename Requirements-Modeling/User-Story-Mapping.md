# User Story Map & Releases

* **MVP :** Courier logs into the web dashboard, registers a parcel, assigns a compartment, and deposits it at the terminal by scanning the Parcel ID; the guest recipient receives an SMS/email code and collects the parcel at the terminal; the administrator monitors occupancy and manages locker availability.
* **Release 1 :** Recipient login & live parcel tracking, lost code recovery, automated 24h/4h expiry reminders, 7-day expiry retrieval, and on-site issue reporting & resolution.
* **Release N :** Automated hardware telemetry alerts, external maintenance team escalation, immutable audit & history logs, offline terminal caching, deadline extensions, and GDPR account erasure.
